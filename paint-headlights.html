Contamination removal

Paint Overspray Removal

Overspray happens more often than most people realize. If your paint or glass feels bumpy and you can see small color specks that didn't come off in the wash, it's most likely overspray. A car wash won't remove it, and left alone it can permanently damage the paint.

We work with construction, pool and paint companies to safely remove overspray from vehicles near painted buildings, homes and pools, plus damage from road paving and road painting. We remove paint, tar and cement, and our methods will not void any warranties.

Every job is quoted per situation and level of contamination. Contact us quickly to avoid further damage.

The removal process

Six steps, with no paint loss.

Pre-clean inspection

We walk the vehicle with you and record existing scratches, dings, chips, swirls and chipped glass.

Wash

Removes surface dirt and adds a protective barrier before any foreign material is lifted.

Contaminant removal

A specially designed product lifts paint dots with no paint loss. No compounding or sanding.

Polish

A mild hand polish clears residue from paint, glass, trim and chrome. Vinyl and convertible tops are scrubbed.

Wax or reseal

We finish with a factory specified polymer based paint sealant.

Final inspection

We walk the finished vehicle with you, and you sign a release.

Overspray questions

What owners ask us.

Is the process going to damage my clear coat?

No. Our processes have been found safe by most major automotive manufacturers and are proven clear coat safe. Overspray usually sits on top of the clear coat.

Do you have a guarantee?

We guarantee the process we use will not harm your finish. We can't guarantee your paint job because we didn't paint your vehicle, but we do stand behind our work.

How long will it take?

Depending on the size and color of the vehicle and how severe the overspray is, a two person crew averages about two to four hours per vehicle.

What about my wax or sealant?

After the overspray is removed we polish the vehicle, then apply a polymer sealant or paste wax.

What about overspray on my windows?

We use a non abrasive system on all glass. It's the safest and most thorough way to clean it.

Headlight questions

Restore or replace?

Restoration or replacement?

Replacement means removing your headlights. Restoration doesn't. Replacing headlights with labor at a dealership can cost between $300 and $1,900.

Why does it matter for safety?

Depending on how deteriorated they are, discolored headlights reduce how well your lighting works and make driving at night less safe. Properly restored headlights look and work like new.

How long does it take?

Most restorations take an hour or less. The protective coating needs one more hour to cure, so allow two hours. Full cure takes about a day, so don't wash or wax the vehicle for 24 hours.

What causes headlights to deteriorate?

Most headlights are polycarbonate, not glass. Pollution, humidity, acid rain, cleaning chemicals, salt, debris and sunlight oxidize the factory coating, and in extreme cases damage the lens itself.
