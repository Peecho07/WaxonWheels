About us

Superior quality for the fine automobiles of South Florida.

We detail for residential clients, dealerships, auto shows, and commercial and management companies. We provide our own soft water and our own electricity, so we can detail or wash virtually anywhere.

Services are tailor made to suit each client's needs. No hidden charges or surprises, and we will never oversell you. We can restore your vehicle to showroom condition, and we pride ourselves on being environmentally conscious.
