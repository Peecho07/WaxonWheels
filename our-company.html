Our Company

Servicing Palm Beach County since 2004

A mobile auto detailing service that comes to your home, your office or your barn.

Wax on Wheels is a unique and professional mobile auto detailing service, located in Wellington, FL just west of West Palm Beach, that will come to your home or office or barn.

We offer our detailing services for residential, dealerships, auto shows, and commercial and management industries. We also offer manpower for any special event. Our detail specialists will take care of business at auto shows, convention centers, auto displays at malls, golf tournaments and more. We work hand in hand with vehicle services coordinators, event services coordinators and automotive management companies.

We are available 7 days a week. We offer superior quality for a mobile auto detailer servicing the fine automobiles of South Florida. We provide our own soft water and our own electricity, so we can detail or wash virtually anywhere.

Services are tailor made to suit each client's needs. No hidden charges or surprises. We will never oversell you.

We can restore your vehicle to showroom condition, and we pride ourselves on being environmentally conscious.
