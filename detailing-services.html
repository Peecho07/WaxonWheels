Paint Overspray Removal

Paint overspray happens more often than most people realize. If you run your hand over your paint and glass and it feels bumpy, and you can see small colour specks that did not come off in the wash, it is most likely overspray. It will not come off with a car wash.

Overspray occurs when paint particles are carried through the air, sometimes for several miles depending on wind, and land on the vehicles below. Left alone it can permanently damage the paint surface. Our cleaning and removal techniques are chosen for the type of finish and contaminant involved, and our methods will not void any warranties.

We work closely with construction companies, pool companies and paint companies in the area to safely remove overspray from vehicles sprayed while painting office buildings, apartment complexes, residential homes and inground pools. We also handle damage from road paving and road painting.

We remove paint, tar and cement. All overspray removal is quoted per situation and per level of contamination, so contact us quickly to avoid further damage.

The removal process

Pre cleaning inspection

We examine the vehicle with the owner and record any existing scratches, dings, rock chips, burns, swirl marks and chipped glass.

Wash

Removes all surface dirt and provides a protective barrier before any foreign material is lifted.

Contaminant removal

A specially designed product gently lifts paint dots and other substances with no paint loss. Unlike compounding or sanding, it does not affect painted surfaces or clear coats.

Polish

Hand rubbing with a mild cleaning polish clears any residue from paint, glass, trim and chrome. Vinyl and convertible tops are carefully scrubbed.

Wax or reseal

The finishing stage. We apply a factory specified polymer based paint sealant.

Final inspection

We walk the completed vehicle with the claimant for re-inspection, at which point the owner is asked to sign a release.

Headlight Restoration

Clouded, yellowing headlight lenses affect millions of cars on the road. They spoil the look of the car, and they can dim your lights enough to become a safety concern at night.

The cause of the yellowing is exposure to sunlight, air pollution, dust, road debris and daily driving. Over time these elements damage the plastic headlight of most modern cars and trucks. The good news is that the damage can be fixed, and we can restore your lights to a like new look instead of replacing them.

Best of all we are mobile. We bring our shop to you.

Headlight restoration is the affordable alternative to replacement. It involves cleaning, sanding and polishing the lens to remove contamination, then applying a clear protective coating to keep the lights looking and performing like new. Properly executed, it restores the original lighting performance and improves the appearance.

We do not replace headlights, and we do not remove them to restore the exterior of the lens.

Replacement requires the removal of your headlights. Restoration does not. Replacing headlights with labour at the dealership can cost between $300 and $1,900.
Depending on the severity of the deterioration, discoloured headlights reduce the effectiveness of your lighting system and create an unsafe environment for the people in the vehicle. Properly restored headlights look and work like new.
In most cases a restoration is completed in an hour or less. The clear protective coating should cure for one additional hour, so please allow two hours for the appointment. Full cure takes about a day, so do not wash or wax the vehicle for 24 hours afterwards.
Most new headlights are polycarbonate rather than glass. Over time pollution, humidity, acid rain, cleaning chemicals, salt, road debris and sunlight oxidise the protective coating the manufacturer applied. In extreme cases the oxidation destroys that coating and damages the surface of the lens itself.
