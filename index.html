Welcome to

Wax On Wheels

Wax On Wheels, established in 2004, is the premier mobile car wash and auto detailing company in Palm Beach County. On board we carry our own treated water and power systems, which lets us detail your vehicle to professional standards anywhere we can park.

Our detail specialists use the highest quality products and give your vehicle superior service. Our detailers are professional, well groomed and uniformed, with many years of knowledge and experience behind them.

Wax On Wheels is licensed and insured. Gift certificates are available for purchase.
